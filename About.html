About Noor Academy

At Noor Academy, our mission is to make learning the Quran and Arabic engaging, accessible, and deeply rewarding for students of all ages. We specialize in teaching the Qur’an with proper Tajweed, recitation, and memorization (Hifz) — helping learners recite the Book of Allah with accuracy, beauty, and understanding.

Our academy also offers comprehensive lessons in Arabic letters (Qaida) and Tajweed rules, ensuring every student builds a strong foundation in pronunciation and reading fluency. We believe that mastering the Qur’an begins with understanding its letters and sounds — and we make that journey interactive, fun, and inspiring through games, visual tools, and personalized attention.

All our teachers are native Arabic speakers from Egypt, graduates of Al-Azhar University, and fluent in English, making communication smooth and effective. They bring not only academic excellence but also a love for the Qur’an, teaching with patience, care, and enthusiasm.

We aim to make authentic Qur’anic education affordable and accessible, offering high-quality instruction to learners around the world — right from the comfort of their homes. At Noor Academy, we don’t just teach reading; we nurture a lifelong connection with the words of Allah.
