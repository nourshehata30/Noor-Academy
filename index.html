About Noor Academy

Noor Academy is dedicated to providing high-quality education in Arabic language and Quranic studies. Our mission is to offer comprehensive and accessible learning experiences for students of all ages, aiming to foster a deep understanding and appreciation of the Arabic language and the Holy Quran.

How We Teach Arabic And Quran

At Noor Academy, we utilize a structured and interactive teaching approach to make learning Arabic and Quranic studies engaging and effective. Our curriculum is designed by experienced educators and scholars who integrate traditional teaching methods with modern educational tools.

Arabic Language Program

-Beginner to Advanced Levels: We offer courses tailored to different proficiency levels, ensuring that each student can start at the right point and progress steadily.

-Interactive Learning: Our classes incorporate multimedia resources,and interactive exercises to ease learning.

Quranic Studies Program

-Tajweed and Recitation: Students learn the rules of Tajweed to recite the Quran with proper pronunciation and intonation, preserving the beauty and authenticity of the recitation.

-Memorization (Hifz): We offer structured memorization programs with personalized support to help students commit the Quran to memory efficiently.
