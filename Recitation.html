Quran Recitation Course

Course Overview

Our Quran Recitation Course is designed to help students of all ages learn how to recite the Holy Quran with correct pronunciation, rhythm, and fluency — following the rules of Tajweed. Through personalized instruction and practice, you'll gain confidence in reading the Quran beautifully and accurately.

Course Structure

  • ✅ Mastery of Arabic letters and their sounds within Quranic context.
  • ✅ Understanding the Tajweed rules — articulation points, elongation, and proper pronunciation.
  • ✅ Learning correct pauses, stops, and flow for smooth recitation.
  • ✅ Guided recitation practice with certified Egyptian teachers.
  • ✅ Weekly progress assessments and recitation correction sessions.

Who Is This Course For?

This course is perfect for students who can already read Arabic letters and wish to improve their recitation skills. We welcome children, adults, and anyone seeking to perfect their Quran recitation with proper Tajweed rules.

Duration & Schedule

Each class lasts 30 to 45 minutes , ensuring focus and engagement without fatigue. Students can choose between 2 to 5 sessions per week , depending on their goals and availability. Our flexible scheduling makes it easy to learn around school or work.
